# What is submitted, what is not, and why

TMLR judges two things: whether the claims are supported by the evidence, and
whether the work would interest some portion of the audience. This file lists
exactly what a reviewer receives against the first of those.

| file | what it is |
|---|---|
| `PAPER.md` | the paper — 11 sections, 22.8k words |
| `fig_c6_forest.png` | Figure 1, the C1→C6 forest plot (§6.5) |
| `fig_stratc_funnel.png` | Figure 2, the repository-sweep funnel (§6.4) |

`PAPER_SHORT.md` is a 7.2k-word condensation kept in sync by `verify_short.py`.

## 3. The artefact

The full repository. Load-bearing directories, all described in `MANIFEST.md`:

    responses/          3,344 model cells — every table is computed from these
    responses_truncated/ quarantined cells, kept so the paper cannot look more complete than it is
    datasets/           all 15 datasets as the experiment used them, SHA256-manifested
    synth/tables/       the 20 Stratum E tables — 113,400 rows, published now that the runs are done
    uci/                the upstream tables Strata B and D are built from
    openml/ kaggle_meta/ hf_meta/ ucimeta/   the repository sweeps behind §4.3 and §6.4

## 4. Reproduction

    python3.14 -m venv .venv && .venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt
    python3 verify_paper.py  > NUMBERS.txt     # regenerate the source of truth
    python3 verify_synth.py                    # regenerate NUMBERS_E.txt

Then eleven checkers, all of which must exit zero:

    verify_tables.py      every table row against its source row      101 rows
    verify_arithmetic.py  every stated relation is self-consistent
    prose_pins.py         every quantity stated in a SENTENCE          28 pins
    claim_audit.py        no decimal appears that NUMBERS lacks
    consistency.py        no stale figure across deliverables
    verify_appendix.py    the appendix delivers what the paper promises
    verify_datasets.py    datasets/ still matches the corpus
    verify_synth.py       Stratum E; refuses to certify a partial roster
    verify_section8.py    every §8 quantity, recomputed from primary sources   75 claims
    verify_short.py       PAPER_SHORT.md agrees with PAPER.md
    verify_env.py         the pin, the interpreter and NUMBERS.txt agree

`verify_section8.py` is deliberately not a regex checker: each claim is written
out as a literal and recomputed from the frozen tables and the response cache,
reading nothing back out of `NUMBERS_E.txt`, so it is a check and not an echo.

## 5. Not submitted

Working files, kept in the repository for provenance but not part of the
submission: `PAPER_DRAFT.md`, `PAPER_OUTLINE.md`, `SECTION3_DRAFT.md`,
`SECTION7_DRAFT.md`, `SECTION7_3_DRAFT.md`, `CLAIM2_DRAFT.md`,
`PENDING_PAPER_EDITS.md`, `OVERNIGHT_SUMMARY.md`, `RESUME_TOMORROW.md`,
`WORKING_scorecard_superseded.md`, `synth/STATUS.md`.

They are drafts and running notes. Several record decisions the paper reports
— `SECTION7_3_DRAFT.md` carries the correction that §7.3's held-out
characterisation had been generalised from two models that turned out to be the
minority — so they are kept rather than deleted, but they are not deliverables.

## 6. Known limits of this submission

* `gemini-3.5-flash` is **provisional wherever it appears**: eight cells
  quarantined for a truncation interaction, documented in §9 and Appendix L.
* The Stratum E tables are now **published**, which permanently spends their
  novelty guarantee. §8's result is a statement about the models as they were
  in August 2026, not a reusable benchmark.
* `PREREG.md` is **not a registration** — no public registry entry, no
  third-party timestamp — and says so in its own words. Two amendments are
  post-hoc, dated, and were committed before the cells they authorise were
  scored.
* Two of §8's controls (the scale ladder, the opaque-name arm) are post-hoc and
  off-roster. They enter no aggregate.
