Why does each of these columns give the answer away?

Every column below comes from a public dataset and has been judged unusable for prediction. Your job is to say why — and whether the evidence shown supports that judgement at all. No expertise is needed; everything required is on each card. About 40 minutes.

Read this first

You are judging one column against one target, at one moment in time — the prediction point, the instant the model is asked for its answer. A value that does not honestly exist yet at that moment, or that exists only because the outcome already happened, is inadmissible.

The four categories

REASON

The target was computed, derived or decided from this column. The label is the downstream thing — change the column and the label changes, by definition.

CONSEQUENCE

The column exists because the outcome occurred. The column is the downstream thing — it would be blank, absent or meaningless had the outcome gone the other way.

TIMING

Neither of the above, but the value is recorded after the moment of prediction. An ordinary measurement, taken too late.

CONTESTED

The quotation genuinely supports both a case for and a case against, and does not settle it. Rare — use it sparingly.

The distinction between the first two is one question: Reason is about how the label was made. Consequence is about how the column was made. Both correlate with the outcome — every leaking column does — so correlation tells you nothing. Ask which way the arrow points.

When two seem to apply

Some columns are genuinely both a trace of the outcome and an input to the label. Check in this order and stop at the first yes:

  1. Does the quotation say the target was computed, derived or decided from this column? → Reason
  2. Else — would the column be blank, absent or meaningless if the outcome had gone the other way? → Consequence
  3. Else — is it simply recorded after the prediction point? → Timing
  4. Else, and only else → Contested

Rules

  • Judge the quotation, not the domain. If the sentence only tells you when a value was recorded, that is Timing even if you suspect something deeper.
  • Work alone. Do not discuss items with anyone else who is coding them.
  • Answer “No” freely on the second question. A disagreement there is more useful than a polite yes.
  • Do not go back and change earlier answers once you spot a pattern.
